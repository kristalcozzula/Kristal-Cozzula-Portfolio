Something more about me

For seven years, I worked as a sales assistant, working closely with people. Every day I listened to what they were looking for, what was putting them in difficulty, and what they couldn't say in words. There I learned to put myself in other people's shoes and turn what I observed into concrete solutions.

From that experience, the desire to do the same in the digital field was born, giving space to my creativity. I chose to study UX/UI design, and today I design clear and accessible interfaces, visual identities, and websites.

I believe a good project is born from dialogue: I listen, propose, and build every step together with the client to create digital experiences that make people's lives easier.

My values

Listening
Every project starts here: before designing, I want to really understand what the client needs.

Empathy
I put myself in the shoes of the people who will use what I create, because good design comes from understanding who is on the other side

Continuous growth
I'm curious and always looking for new stimuli: I like to update myself and discover different tools, because in design you never stop learning.

Spirit of initiative
When faced with something new, I don't stand still: if I don't know how to do something, I think, try, and find the solution!

My interests

Off-screen, I continue to create: crochet is my way of shaping ideas with my hands, with patience and attention to detail.

I feed on cinema, music, and history podcasts that fuel my curiosity.

I seek silence and inspiration in nature excursions, during which I willingly stop to observe my surroundings, including animals, and photograph them.

My design philosophy

  • I believe a successful interface needs no explanation.
  • For me, functionality and aesthetics go together: a beautiful design must also work.
  • I design so everyone can use what I create, not just those who are more experienced.
  • I focus on the people who will use the product, not my personal taste.
  • I think the best results come from talking to the customer, one step at a time.
