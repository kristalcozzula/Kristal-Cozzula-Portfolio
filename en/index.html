How I work?

Every project starts with listening and grows together with the client. I involve them at every stage with continuous briefings, so the design choices remain aligned with their tastes and his needs. Whether it's a website, a logo, or a visual identity, I always work the same way: I start by listening and build each step together with the customer, to arrive at a result that truly reflects their needs and those of their audience.

01

I listen

I understand the customer, the industry, and the public through briefings and analysis of competitors.

02

I ideate and propose

I present more proposals to choose from and develop them step by step.

03

I compare

I finalize the design based on customer feedback and deliver it after a final review.

Here's how I applied this approach to a real estate agency's website, which had a dated, anonymous, and poorly functional design. After listening to the customer and analyzing the competitors and the audience, I proposed several solutions and shared the design at each step. The result is a modern and more functional site, approved together before going online.
